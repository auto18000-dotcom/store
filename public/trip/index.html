Trip Goals · five weighted criteria

Optimize the trip around what matters to you

Weigh these against each other, and Check will measure your plan against them — facts only, never a score for the day itself.

Proximity & efficiency

Stops close together, in an order that doesn't double back.

Cost effectiveness

The same kind of experience for less, where a fit exists.

Health & fitness

Walking within your comfort, rain-safe plans, meals on time.

Culinary & cultural

A local food experience and a cultural highlight most days.

Trip-specific must-dos

Things you named for this trip get a place, and pinned plans never move.
