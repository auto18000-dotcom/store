About this hotel

A modern Accor-brand hotel built into the Gare de Lyon TGV station itself, so a train platform is closer than the nearest metro stop most other hotels can offer. Rooms are compact and quiet for a station location, with a rooftop terrace looking over the tracks and the Paris skyline beyond.
